MCP — the Model Context Protocol — gives AI applications a standard way to interact with your product.

It was proposed as an open standard by Anthropic in November 2024, adopted across the AI industry in 2025, and is now under the vendor-neutral Linux Foundation.

As of October 2026, the official registry lists 45,000 MCP servers.

And the shift is already showing up in how people use the internet. Adobe found that traffic from AI assistants to U.S. retail websites grew more than tenfold in less than two years.

So this isn't betting on some proprietary integration. It's supporting an emerging standard for how agents use software.
