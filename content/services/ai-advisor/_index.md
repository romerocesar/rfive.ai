AI Advisor helps business and operational leaders decide where AI can create real leverage and move from scattered ideas to focused action.

## Who It Helps

This service is for founders, executives, and functional leaders who need a clear AI strategy before investing heavily in tools, vendors, prototypes, or hiring. You do not need to become an AI expert to lead your organization well or help your teams become more productive. We explain capabilities, limitations, and tradeoffs in plain language so you can replace pressure and uncertainty with informed, confident decisions.

## What We Do

- Understand your team's pain points and improve their understanding of what AI can realistically do, where it falls short, and how to evaluate new claims and tools.
- Identify AI opportunities that address those pain points and create practical business value.
- Capture the value your team expects from each opportunity, including time saved, better decisions, improved service, or new revenue.
- Estimate the likely cost, complexity, feasibility, and risk of pursuing each opportunity.
- Prioritize opportunities by comparing your team's value estimates with R5's independent cost estimates, then align on what to pursue now, revisit later, or avoid.

## Typical Outcomes

- A sharper AI strategy tied to business priorities.
- A ranked list of opportunities with explicit value and cost assumptions.
- Clear recommendations for build, buy, partner, or pause decisions.
- A more informed and confident team that can discuss AI without hype or unnecessary pressure.
- Shared language and decision criteria across leadership and functional stakeholders.
