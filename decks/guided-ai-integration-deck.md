### The AI Blueprint for Non-Tech Businesses

A practical five-step method for leaders who need clarity, not complexity.

It creates a shared understanding of business value and implementation effort—so the right opportunities rise to the top.

> **Visual:** Five connected stages showing the journey from “Anxiety” to “Productivity.”

## Slide 4: Workflows in Practice

### Client Follow-Up and CRM

**Before:** Switching among email, CRM, and spreadsheets; writing every follow-up from scratch; leads added inconsistently.

**Automated workflow:** GPT connects with Gmail and Attio to draft personalized follow-ups and add leads to the CRM.

**Practical result:** More leads captured, more timely follow-ups, less manual writing, and communication that retains the client's voice.

### Continuous Call Improvement

**Before:** Meeting insights depend on manual notes and lessons are easy to lose before the next client conversation.

**Automated workflow:** Meeting capture produces notes, then deeper analysis identifies patterns and specific ways to improve the next call.

**Practical result:** A repeatable learning loop after every client call. This workflow is used by me and by at least one client.

### Presenter notes

- Say: AI becomes useful when it disappears into workflows people already understand.
- Make the point: These are not generic demonstrations. Each workflow begins with a recurring task, its inputs and outputs, and the services already in use.
- Then: Guided AI Integration applies this same approach to one high-impact opportunity in your business.
