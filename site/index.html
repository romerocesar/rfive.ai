A few practical questions

Before we connect.

Does our product need an API?

An existing API is the best starting point. We review what it exposes and what your users need to do. If something is missing, we identify the API work needed before committing to the integration.

Which AI agents will it work with?

We target MCP-compatible clients and test the specific clients your users need. Transport, authentication, and tool support vary between clients, so we agree on a compatibility scope rather than promise every agent will work out of the box.

How do you handle permissions and actions?

We design authorization around your product’s access model, validate inputs, and expose only the agreed capabilities. For sensitive actions such as purchases or bookings, we design confirmation steps and enforce the required checks in the integration—not just in an agent’s instructions.

Where does the server run, and who manages it?

We agree on the hosting environment and operational responsibilities during scoping. R5 provides ongoing management within that agreement, including maintenance and compatibility work. Hosting costs, monitoring, support hours, and any service commitments are defined before launch.

What will it cost, and how long will it take?

That depends on your API, the workflows, authentication requirements, and management needs. Start with a free consultation. We’ll discuss the fit and next steps, then define a scope and proposal before development begins.
